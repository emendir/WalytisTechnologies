# Block Validity

A block's validity is decided by each peer individually.
They may need to gather further information (ancestors) in order to reach this decision.
A block's validity is a deterministic property of only its contents, the applicable protocol rules, and its referenced ancestry, and is independent of children blocks, the state of the network, or claims of other peers.

## Block Validity Criteria

A valid Block MUST be composed of the constituents specified in [Block Constituents — Overview](6.%20Block%20Constituents%20Overview.md).

A valid Block's identifier MUST be decodable as specified in [Block Identifier](7.%20Block%20Constituent%20Definitions.md#Block%20Identifier), and the decoded data MUST be consistent with the block fields.

A valid Block's constituents MUST satisfy the definitions and constraints specified in [Block Constituent — Definitions and Constraints](7.%20Block%20Constituent%20Definitions.md).

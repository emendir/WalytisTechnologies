## Guarantees

The block content associated with a block identifier WILL NOT ever change over time.

A block WILL NOT ever be removed from the blockchain.

A valid block WILL eventually become available to every participating peer, subject to eventual network connectivity and continued participation by at least one peer possessing the block.

## Limitations

Any given peer might not have the newest blocks at a given point in time.


The Walytis Blockchain Database does not provide any confidentiality or authenticity guarantees.

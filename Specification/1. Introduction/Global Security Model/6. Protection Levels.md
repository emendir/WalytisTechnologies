# Protection Levels

A **protection level** specifies the strength of protection applied to data.
Unlike a [confidentiality classification](5.%20Confidentiality%20Classification.md#Confidentiality%20Classification), a protection level describes a security property of the system's handling of data rather than the sensitivity and consequences of disclosure.

The protection level describes the **strength of the barrier against disclosure**, while the confidentiality classification describes the **consequences if that barrier is defeated**.

The system defines the following protection levels:

### No Protection

Data is not intentionally protected against disclosure to outside observers.

Data may be transmitted or stored in a form that is directly observable by an outside observer.

### Standard Protection

Data is actively protected against disclosure to outside observers, but does not require the strongest protection mechanisms available to the system.

Standard Protection MUST prevent an outside observer from obtaining the data through ordinary observation of network traffic or other channels covered by the relevant protocol.

### Strong Protection

Data is actively protected using the strongest protection mechanisms required by the system.

Strong Protection MUST be designed to resist unauthorised disclosure even in circumstances in which Standard Protection would be insufficient.

## Relationship Between Confidentiality and Protection

Confidentiality classification and protection level describe different properties and MUST NOT be treated as interchangeable.

The confidentiality classification describes **how harmful disclosure would be**. The protection level describes **how strongly the system protects the data against disclosure**.

The recommended relationship between the two is:

| Confidentiality classification | Minimum protection level |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------ |
| Exposed                        | No Protection            |
| Discreet                       | Standard Protection      |
| Secret                         | Strong Protection        |

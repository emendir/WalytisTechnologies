## Interface

## Guarantees

Each block in an identity's underlying blockchain is authenticated by another identity who is, according to the state of block's ancestry, a controller of the identity.

## Limitations

Peers in an Identity's observers list and not in the Identity's members list cannot share blocks among each other. They can only receive new blocks from peers in the Identity's members list.

On first contact, peers SHOULD verify each others identities out-of-band, because the genesis key incorporated into an Identity's identifier is assumed to weaken over time.

# Required Services

## Walytis Blockchain-Database

### Interface

WalytisIdentities uses the interface specified in [Walytis Blockchain — Provided Services — Interface](../3.%20Walytis%20Blockchain/2.%20Provided%20Services/1.%20Blockchain.md#Interface).

### Requirements

 WalytisIdentities relies on the guarantees specified in [Walytis Blockchain — Provided Services — Guarantees](../3.%20Walytis%20Blockchain/2.%20Provided%20Services/1.%20Blockchain.md#Guarantees).

The Walytis Blockchain Database MUST NOT communicate with other peers independently of the Networking Module.

### Limitations

 WalytisIdentities is designed to utilise the Walytis Blockchain subject to the limitations specified in [Walytis Blockchain — Provided Services — Limitations](../3.%20Walytis%20Blockchain/2.%20Provided%20Services/1.%20Blockchain.md#Limitations).

## Why no read-only blockchain access?

A WalytisNetworking manager could be configured to use different lists of identities allowed for sending blocks to and for receiving blocks from, where the list of identities to send to is a superset of the group to receive from, creating a group of read-only identities and a group of read/write identities.

This would have the inherent limitation that identities in the read-only group cannot share blocks with each other.
More seriously, when a controller is removed from an an identity, that controller could maliciously pass blocks to pure observers before they hear of his removal. They accept his blocks, but since they are never able to share them, those blocks never propagate to the rest of the network.

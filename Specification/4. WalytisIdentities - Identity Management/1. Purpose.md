# Purpose

In Endra's model for secure communications, the core entities in question are _identities_.

The technology used to provide authenticity and confidentiality is asymmetric cryptography.
Asymmetric cryptography requires identities to hold private keys which they must keep secret and public keys which they must share with peers they wish to communicate with.

The purpose of WalytisIdentities is to create and operate identities based on asymmetric cryptography in the context of a peer-to-peer network, overcoming the challenges inherent in [the assumptions about the required services](#Assumptions), such as asynchronous communication, incomplete local blockchain state, intermittent peer availability, and the eventual compromise or obsolescence of cryptographic keys and algorithms.

# Required Services

### Walytis Blockchain Database

Walytis is a distributed database with the architecture and guarantees of a blockchain.
A unique Walytis database serves as the foundation for every identity.
The state of an identity at a given moment is determined solely from the available data on the database at that moment.
No data of [Confidentiality Level Secret](../1.%20Introduction/Global%20Security%20Model/5.%20Confidentiality%20Classification.md#Secret) is stored there.
The security guarantees the blockchain lie solely in the realm of data integrity, NOT confidentiality or authenticity.

#### Assumptions

- Any given peer may not have the newest blocks.
- A valid block will eventually become available to every participating peer, subject to eventual network connectivity and continued participation by at least one peer possessing the block.
- The block content associated with a block identifier will never change.
- A block will never be removed from the blockchain.
- The Walytis Blockchain Database does not provide any confidentiality or authenticity guarantees.
- The Walytis Blockchain Database MUST NOT communicate with other peers outside of the Networking Module.

### Networking Module

The networking module uses an identity's encryption and authentication capabilities to securely share the blocks produced by the Walytis Database Blockchain with other peers defined by the identity.
It also provides communication channels of [Strong Protection](../1.%20Introduction/Global%20Security%20Model/6.%20Protection%20Levels.md#Strong%20Protection) for securely sharing data of [Confidentiality Level Secret](../1.%20Introduction/Global%20Security%20Model/5.%20Confidentiality%20Classification.md#Secret), such as private keys.

#### Assumptions
- Blocks MUST only be shared with the defined set of peers with at least [Standard Protection](../1.%20Introduction/Global%20Security%20Model/6.%20Protection%20Levels.md#Standard%20Protection).

### Cryptography

#### Assumptions

- Cryptographic keys remain secure for a limited time, where that limitation is ill-defined.
- A cryptographic algorithm might come to be considered insecure at any point in time.



# Operation Environment

### Network

#### Assumptions

- Not all peers are online at all times.
- For any given pair of peers, it is not guaranteed that they will ever be online simultaneously.
- For any given pair of peers $A$ and $Z$, there will often be a chain of asynchronous peer connections between them. For example, $A$ connecting to $B$, $B$ later connecting to $C$ and $C$ later connecting to $Z$ constitutes such a chain of asynchronous peer connections.

### Local Execution Environment

The WalytisIdentities implementation operates within an environment that provides persistent local execution, storage, networking, and cryptographic capabilities.

The requirements in this section describe assumptions made by the implementation about its execution environment. They do not prescribe a particular operating system, filesystem, hardware platform, or network technology.
#### Assumption

- Local storage is persistent and provides at least [Standard Protection](../1.%20Introduction/Global%20Security%20Model/6.%20Protection%20Levels.md#Standard%20Protection).
- The execution environment provides [Strong Protection](../1.%20Introduction/Global%20Security%20Model/6.%20Protection%20Levels.md#Strong%20Protection).
- The local clock is unreliable regarding:
	- synchronicity with other peers 
	- regular time-keeping

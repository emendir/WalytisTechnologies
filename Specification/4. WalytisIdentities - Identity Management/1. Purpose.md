# Purpose

In Endra's model for secure communications, the most fundamental entity is the _identity.

The technology used to provide authenticity and confidentiality is asymmetric cryptography.
Asymmetric cryptography requires identities to hold private keys which they must keep secret and public keys which they must share with peers they wish to communicate with.

The purpose of WalytisIdentities is to create and operate identities based on asymmetric cryptography in the context of a peer-to-peer network, overcoming the challenges inherent in [the limitations assumed about the required services](#Limitations), such as asynchronous communication, incomplete local blockchain state, intermittent peer availability, and the eventual compromise or obsolescence of cryptographic keys and algorithms.
